Dermato ("we", "our", "the app") provides AI-assisted skin analysis and connects patients with dermatologists for review and follow-up. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled, across both the Dermato mobile app and the Dermato web app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: full name, phone number, email address, age, and skin type, when you register.
  • Skin photos: images you capture or upload for analysis (front, and optionally left/right angles on the web app), along with the AI-generated severity assessment and recommendations for each photo.
  • Health/treatment history: treatment plans, recheck schedules, your optional skin history (allergies, current products, known conditions, medications), and any notes you or your dermatologist add to a scan.
  • Messages: messages exchanged between you and your assigned dermatologist within the app.
  • Appointments: if you book a consultation, the date, time, and dermatologist you booked with.
  • Device push token (mobile app only): a device identifier used solely to deliver notifications (e.g. recheck reminders, new messages) to your phone. You can decline notification permission at any time; the app works without it.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app, and we do not sell your information.

How we use this information

  • To run the AI skin analysis and show you your results and progress over time.
  • To share your scans, history, and messages with the dermatologist assigned to your care, so they can review your case and respond to you.
  • To send you recheck reminders and message notifications (by email and, on the mobile app, push notification).
  • To operate your account (login, password reset).

Who can see your information

Your skin photos, treatment history, and messages are visible to the dermatologist assigned to your case, and to administrators of the Dermato platform for the purpose of operating the service. We do not share your information with outside companies or advertisers.

Camera and photo access

The app requests camera and/or photo library access only when you choose to take or upload a photo for analysis. We only store the specific photo you choose to submit — the app does not access your camera roll in the background.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and scan history for as long as your account is active, so you and your dermatologist can track progress over time. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us using the details below.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, not in plain text. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Children

Dermato is not directed at children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes.
